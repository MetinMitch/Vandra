Shipping and Return

At Vandra, we strive to provide a seamless shopping experience, ensuring you receive your products promptly and can shop with confidence.

Shipping

All Vandra products are fulfilled by Amazon (FBA). When you place an order, Amazon handles the entire process — from warehousing to delivery. Shipping times, options, and fees are determined by Amazon at checkout based on your location and selected delivery method.

• Standard delivery is typically completed within 2–5 business days within the continental U.S.

• Expedited and priority options may be available at checkout.

• Tracking information will be provided via Amazon once your order ships.

If you have any issues with shipping or delivery, please contact Amazon Customer Servicedirectly through your order history.

 

Returns

Vandra products purchased through Amazon are subject to Amazon’s return and refund policies. Most items are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in original condition.

To initiate a return:

1. Go to Your Orders on your Amazon account

2. Select the item you wish to return

3. Follow the on-screen instructions to process the return through Amazon

If your item arrives damaged or defective, Amazon will typically offer a replacement or full refund. For assistance, please contact Amazon Support through your account.

 

Questions or Issues?

If you experience a product-related issue (such as a missing part or safety concern), you can also reach out to us directly at info@vandrastudios.com. Please include your Amazon order number and a description of the issue so we can assist further.
